# Lifecycle workflows

This is behavioral design guidance, not code. Requirements explicitly stated in the business brief are approved requirements; every workflow detail and suggested state name below is a proposal until approved. Tax, legal, accounting, refund, retention and approval policies remain unresolved where noted in [../OPEN_DECISIONS.md](../OPEN_DECISIONS.md).

## Account and checkout
Implementation clarification (2026-11-03): customer delivery charges are configured per Egyptian governorate in the existing `settings` JSON record (`group=shipping`, `key=governorate_rates`). No default rate is assumed; placing an order is blocked when the chosen governorate has no explicit configured rate. The selected rate is included in the signed checkout quote and copied to the order and revision, so later rate changes do not rewrite old orders. Initial website order status is `new`; payment is a separate pending COD record. Tax is not calculated and `tax_total` remains nullable until tax policy is approved. For this MVP checkout, the address snapshot is shipping-only. `subtotal` is the sum of charged line totals after any product sale; `discount_total` records the difference from regular prices and is informational, not subtracted a second time. Grand total is subtotal + customer shipping charge; no coupon or tax is applied.
Laravel web session authenticates users. A visitor may browse, choose size/color variants, add them to cart, and open/view the checkout page without an account. Require registration/login only before final order placement. Persist the guest cart under its opaque guest token and keep selected variants and quantities; preserve any entered checkout-form choices in protected server-side session state while sending the visitor through authentication. After successful authentication, rotate the session ID, transactionally attach/merge the guest cart to the customer, preserve all distinct items, merge quantities for duplicate variants, and return the customer to checkout with choices restored. Revalidate product availability and current regular/sale prices after authentication; if the total changed since the customer last saw it, display the new total and require explicit confirmation before placing the order. Save address rows only after customer identity is established; snapshot the confirmed address into the order revision. Website checkout offers COD only. A product marked unavailable cannot be checked out; this status applies to all variants. In one transaction create order with `channel=website`, immutable line/name/SKU/variant/regular-price/sale-price/charged-price/discount/currency snapshots, shipping/billing address snapshots and revision 1. Cart prices are not authoritative. Order state is independent of payment, shipment and return states.

## Product price management
Only a user granted the administrator price-management permission may change a product variant's regular or sale price. Prices are maintained separately for each sellable variant/size. A null sale price means no discount; when present it must be lower than the regular price. Each edit appends a price-history version with actor, time and reason; it never updates historical orders. The storefront shows both prices while the sale price is set. Product-wide bulk editing, automatic sale dates, and the exact cost-plus-profit calculation/rounding remain subject to their open decisions.

## Changed order and explicit approval
Any changed price, product or quantity creates a new revision with immutable normalized lines, shipping/billing address snapshots, currency, discounts, customer shipping charge, tax fields where applicable, and final payable total. Suggested revision states: draft → pending_customer → approved/rejected/expired; a later revision can supersede an earlier pending one; application of the approved revision is separately recorded. Persist proposed_at, expires_at (only if expiry policy is approved), decided_at, applied_at. Each customer decision references exact revision ID, actor/customer, channel, timestamp and evidence. It cannot be interpreted as approval of another revision. Pending content cannot be edited; create a new revision. Lock order/revision when assigning a number, deciding, expiring, superseding or applying. Explicit approval for changed price/product/quantity is approved requirement; expiration duration, channels, evidence and rejection/timeout result remain open decisions. Silence is never approval.

## Product supplier assignment and availability
A product may be linked to multiple suppliers (factory/showroom) through `product_suppliers`. Staff maintains each supplier option’s current cost and availability; changes append `product_supplier_history`. For each ordered product line, staff selects the suitable linked supplier and records its cost/name snapshot, actor, time and reason in `order_item_supplier_selections`. Re-selection appends a new event. This is supplier selection only; no purchase order, receipt, payable or inventory movement is created. Separately, staff sets the product-level storefront status to available or unavailable; this gates all its variants and is not a stock count. Each status change is recorded in product availability history. A supplier choice is internal unless it changes customer-facing price/product/quantity, which requires the exact order revision to be approved.

## Payments
Keep payment record and immutable attempts/transactions. Website order payment method is COD. InstaPay is a staff-only method on manually entered Facebook/Instagram orders. Creation requires a proof image, records the staff creator, and stores the generated image path on the private local disk outside `public`; no browser-visible public URL is persisted. Only staff with order-management permission may view or decide a proof. Upload starts pending and never marks payment paid. Acceptance is a deliberate staff decision: update the proof review projection, set payment paid and append a successful verification transaction plus order/activity audit events atomically. Rejection requires a reason, marks only that proof rejected, appends a failed verification transaction and audit events, and leaves payment unpaid/pending. A replacement proof may be added after rejection; all submitted proofs and decisions remain in history, and only one proof may be pending review per payment. InstaPay is not selectable for website channel. Payment state never implicitly changes order/shipment states. No payment-gateway integration is implied. The exact policy for mismatched/partial transfer amounts remains open; do not treat an unreviewed or mismatched transfer as paid.

## Shipping and carrier settlement
**Implemented MVP workflow (S2-07):** Keep shipping companies as manual records; deactivate instead of deleting. Staff may assign a carrier, create an outbound attempt, or update shipment status only while the order status is `shipped`; the order must also have an applied revision/address snapshot and not be closed or received. Retries are allowed after a prior attempt is cancelled or returned to sender; a delivered order cannot be shipped again. A shipment starts at `picked_up` because the order reaches `shipped` after handoff to the carrier; creation records that handoff time and actor. Every subsequent permitted status change adds a timestamped event with actor and optional location/note. Record actual carrier cost independently from the customer shipping charge. The customer charge on an attempt is an allocation of the order's existing customer shipping fee and cannot exceed its unallocated balance, so retries do not automatically charge the customer twice. Expected COD is the unpaid order total less amounts already collected; a verified InstaPay order expects zero COD. On delivery, enter actual collected amount; the page displays actual minus expected as a variance. All money stays DECIMAL. Shipment status/events do not change order or payment status and do not record a carrier settlement.

Full carrier settlement/remittance accounting is deferred: do not infer fees, deductions, cash receipt, remittance, customer liability or refund from shipment data. The event note can retain context about a manually entered discrepancy, but a formal explained/unexplained variance approval workflow, carrier statement fees/deductions, partial remittance and cash transactions require policy and scope approval. Keep customer charge, carrier cost, expected COD, actual collection, carrier remittance and variance conceptually separate.

## Product reviews
Customer submits a product rating and optional comment. Store as pending and hidden from public product pages. Dashboard admin approves or rejects, then independently sets visibility to visible/hidden; only approved + visible reviews appear publicly. The admin can hide a previously visible review without deleting it; append an `activity_logs` entry for every approval/rejection and visibility change. Whether verified purchase is mandatory, edit/re-review behavior and rating scale are policy decisions still open.

## General customer feedback
Customers may send general feedback about the store/service, separately from a product rating and a support request. Store it in `customer_feedback` for dashboard review and internal staff notes. Admin approves or rejects each submission and independently controls whether approved feedback is visible on the website; `show_on_homepage` controls homepage placement. Hide/unhide actions preserve the submission and are recorded in `activity_logs`. Whether guests may submit and whether a customer name is displayed publicly remain open decisions.

## Homepage imagery
Dashboard staff manage homepage design images through `banners`: choose placement/slot, upload or replace the image, set explicit display order, and activate/deactivate it. Staff also controls the enabled state and order of predefined `homepage_sections`, plus Arabic/English headings and text. Section types/settings are validated; this is not a free-form page builder. Home sections can refer to curated product collections, banner placements, or the `home_testimonials` slider. Each curated testimonial stores a generated image path, bilingual public name/message, explicit order, and active flag in the section's JSON settings; dashboard create/update/removal actions are recorded in `activity_logs`. This slider is separate from moderated customer-submitted feedback and product reviews. Optional banner start/end times control banner visibility. Record creator/updater and audit changes. Do not infer “latest” from insertion order. Whether desktop/mobile variants need separate assets remains open.

Category artwork is uploaded and replaced from the category dashboard form. Store only the generated public-storage path in `categories.image_path`; the homepage category tile uses that artwork and does not infer a cover from an arbitrary product. A missing category image is allowed and renders the designed fallback. Failed database updates remove newly uploaded files; replaced or explicitly removed files are deleted after the transaction succeeds. Category image paths are included in category activity snapshots.

## Order audit, follow-up and closure
At creation, save `created_by_user_id` and append a `created` event; for website orders this identifies the authenticated customer user and for social orders the staff operator. Approved order statuses progress sequentially `new` → `confirmed` → `shipped` → `received`. `received` is the successful terminal status and requires staff confirmation of customer receipt; record success in that `status_changed` event and set the queryable closure projection (`close_outcome=success`, `closed_at`) without introducing a separate `closed` status. A failed closure is a separate projection, allowed from any non-received, not-already-failed-closed status; it leaves the operational status unchanged and requires reason code `customer_cancelled`, `customer_refused`, `customer_unreachable`, `incorrect_address`, `product_unavailable`, `duplicate_order`, or `other`; `other` also requires explanatory text. Suppress status transitions while failure-closed. Reopening clears only the current failure projection and leaves the operational status as-is; preserve closure and reopening events. Payment and shipment states remain independent. Every transition, assignment/follow-up, operational note, closure and reopening appends an immutable `order_activity_events` row with actor (NULL only for system), timestamp, previous/current order status, reason and details. Assignment, next follow-up and note are optional fields saved by one dashboard action; append one audit event for each field actually changed. Append events and update projections in one transaction while locking the order row. A refusal/non-receipt is a failed order closure reason; a defect exchange after delivery is recorded as a linked exchange case/return, not as failed delivery. Correct closure errors by reopening/compensating events, never by erasing history.

## Return and refund
**Owner-approved current eligibility:** accept a return only when the product is damaged or has a clear manufacturing defect, and it is returned unused. Record the received condition and inspection decision. The owner bears shipping costs for accepted returns, customer non-receipt/failed delivery, and exchanges; record these as costs against the original order and do not charge them to the customer by default.

After successful delivery, a qualifying damaged-return request reopens the same order under a `damaged_return` after-sales workflow. Preserve the original `received` event, order revision and sale snapshot; reopening starts a new service cycle and does not create a new order or sale. Arrange a `customer_return` shipment to collect the unused damaged/defective product, record receipt and inspection, then create an `exchange` outbound shipment for the replacement and track it through delivery. S2-08 must authorize these case shipments while the original order is `received`; S2-07's normal shipment gate must remain restricted to orders in `shipped`. Add replacement shipping costs to the original order expenses. If an exchange changes customer-facing price, product or quantity, apply the explicit customer-approval requirement; exact line/price-difference mechanics remain open.

Refunds remain manually recorded and linked to the return, original order and payment. The owner has not approved refund amount calculations, payout timing/tender, or review evidence and authorization, so do not infer or automate those policies. No inventory/restocking movement is modeled in this scope; preserve returned condition and disposition for audit.

## Contact Us and customer support
Contact Us accepts a standalone customer/guest message and records the sender details as submitted. A staff member may mark it handled or convert it to a support ticket. Tickets can be linked to a customer and optionally an order; preserve requester contact snapshots. Staff can assign, change priority/status, reply, and resolve/reopen. Customer replies and staff replies are customer-visible; internal notes are explicitly marked internal and excluded from the customer view. Store attachments privately. Append ticket events for each status/assignment/priority/resolution action. Generate persisted unread in-dashboard notifications when a ticket needs staff action; show them when staff open the dashboard and mark read when opened/acknowledged. No realtime push is required.

## Sales, returns, damage and profitability reports
Provide weekly, monthly and yearly views from durable source records. Monthly periods run from day 1 through month end; weeks run Saturday through Friday; calendar boundaries use `Africa/Cairo`. Historical sales use the applied immutable order revision and its numeric product/variant, quantity, regular and sale price snapshots, charged price/discount, customer shipping charge, tax and payable snapshots; never read today's catalog price/name to reconstruct a past order. Show product revenue and customer shipping charges as separate totals, alongside discounts, cancelled/failed orders, returns/refunds and damaged/defective quantity/value, so percentage denominators are visible. Profit breakdown lists product cost and actual/estimated inbound delivery, carrier shipping (including return-to-sender/failed delivery), packaging and advertising costs separately, with estimates excluded when superseded by actual entries and reversals applied. For each catalog price version, preserve factory + inbound delivery + packaging + advertising costs and calculate the displayed markup rate as (regular price − total cost) / total cost × 100, rounded half-up to four decimal places. The administrator manually sets the regular price; the percentage does not calculate or change it. A zero total cost cannot produce a rate. A staff-entered optional sale price is lower than regular price and the storefront displays both. Customer shipping is charged separately. Show contribution as an estimate until accounting treatment for tax, COD timing, refunds, damaged stock and shared advertising is approved.

## Dashboard statistics
Suggested dashboard cards: today's created-order count and actionable queues for customer confirmation/approval, InstaPay proof review, ready to ship, in transit/delivery attempted, delivered, failed/closed, open return/exchange cases, and tickets awaiting staff action. Map these cards to the final approved order/payment/shipment statuses during schema implementation. Counts use each entity's own lifecycle and are clearly labeled; queues may overlap (for example, delivered orders with an unresolved ticket). Delivered uses shipment delivery events, not merely order creation or payment state. Keep date filters in Cairo time and expose the underlying order list for each count. Additional cards may show product revenue, shipping charges, refunds, damage/write-offs and estimated contribution; metric formulas and percentage denominators must remain explicit.

## Cash, expenses and profitability
Cash transactions are append-only in/out entries. Cash sessions record opening, expected and counted closing and variance; adjustments use new transaction. Record factory/product cost from the approved product price history and preserve the cost used for each order item separately from operating costs. No supplier invoice, payable, purchase receipt or inventory valuation is implied. Operating categories confirmed for reporting are shipping, packaging and advertising. Link direct order expenses explicitly; shared advertising remains a general expense unless a future approved attribution rule assigns it to orders. Shared procurement-cost allocation is outside the current schema. Keep operating costs separate and do not invent per-product allocation for shared expenses. Report product cost and operating costs per order, but the exact definition of “net profit” (tax, returns, timing, and shared ad treatment) is not settled; label reports as estimates until approved.

## Transaction boundaries
Transactions cover checkout, revision/approval transition, coupon redemption, payment event posting, carrier settlement, return/refund, cash close and expense posting. Lock aggregate and projection rows, use idempotency tokens, and keep network operations out of SQL transactions.

## S2-08 implementation workflow addendum (2026-11-04)

After a successfully received order, staff opens a damaged-return case on the same order. The order's current status becomes damaged_return, but its prior closed_at/close_outcome, received event and applied order revision are preserved. The original order remains the sale record. If the case is rejected/cancelled or resolved by exchange/refund, current status returns to received; append order activity and return audit events for each transition.

Only damage or a clear manufacturing defect is an eligible reason, and the received product must be unused. Staff approval is followed by a manual customer-return pickup shipment. Delivery of that shipment records item_received; staff then records an inspection note and confirms unused condition before accepting. Inspection rejection requires a reason and does not restock anything.

For the implemented exchange default, the outbound replacement uses the same product variant and quantity as the returned order line, and that option must still be active, published and available. The exact historical product/SKU/variant/quantity are snapshotted on return_items. A different variant/product/quantity or price is blocked because explicit customer approval is already a requirement; no substitute workflow is implemented. The replacement shipment uses the same order and is owner-paid: customer_shipping_charge and COD fields are zero. Delivery completes the return case and restores the order's current status to received.

Return/exchange carrier actual costs are kept distinct from customer shipping and written to the original order cost ledger. On an actual carrier-cost correction, append a linked negative reversal then the corrected actual amount. Product line costs are manually entered as a total amount for that line quantity and are separate from expenses. Actual direct operating cost entries mirror into expenses; estimates stay only in the order-cost ledger. Corrections never delete the original entry. Use the ledger as the direct-cost reporting source so linked expense mirrors are not counted twice.

A staff-reviewed refund amount is recorded as pending; it is not evidence of payout. A separately authorized action marks the refund completed only after staff confirms the amount was sent. A pending mistake may be voided with its reason preserved and then re-entered. The total of non-void refunds is application-capped at the order total. The app does not select the refund tender, calculate product/shipping shares, send money automatically or claim tax/accounting treatment. Refund tender/timing, evidence and approval authority remain owner decisions.

Return-case state transitions, order status changes, cost posts/reversals, refund records and shipment events run transactionally with row locks. Case and shipment create/update forms use replay protection; return quantity validation locks the order and checks that non-rejected/non-cancelled return quantities do not exceed the applied order-revision line quantity. Do not change the S2-07 normal shipment gate: return-case legs are authorized by their linked case while the order is in damaged_return.
