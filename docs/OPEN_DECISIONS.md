# Open business and data decisions

## Status key

- **Approved requirements** are only the business requirements explicitly stated in the user brief and repeated in [database/README.md](database/README.md). They are not accounting/legal policy.
- **Proposed defaults** below are design recommendations for review. None has been accepted unless the user explicitly says so.
- **Unresolved decisions** are questions without a selected policy; schema fields preserve facts without deciding their legal/accounting interpretation.

## Approved requirements (not open)

MySQL; Laravel/Blade modular monolith; Arabic and English; EGP default; customer account required before placing a website order; guests may browse, add to cart and view checkout; Laravel session admin authentication; simple and variable products; product-family SKU generated from the English product name on creation and stable afterward, plus unique sellable variant SKU; products may have multiple factory/showroom supplier options, with a supplier selected per order line; product-level manually set available/unavailable status; no procurement or inventory modules; manual shipping initially with future carrier integrations; DECIMAL money; historical snapshots; explicit approval when final order price/products/quantities change; independent order/payment/shipment statuses; preserved financial and operational audit trail; transaction/row locking for concurrency; no hard deletion of historical financial/operational records.

Latest clarified supplier scope: each product may link to multiple factory/showroom suppliers and staff selects the suitable supplier for each order line. The application does not manage purchase orders, receipts, payables or inventory. Product-level storefront availability is manually set to available/unavailable; website payment is COD only; InstaPay is a staff-entered option only for Facebook/Instagram orders and includes a proof image plus payment verification status; product rating/comment is moderated in the dashboard before publication; track per-order product cost and shipping, packaging and advertising operating costs.

These do not approve a tax rate/treatment, legal retention period, accounting method, refund eligibility, approval expiry/channel, or any other policy listed below.

Additional confirmed operational requirements: homepage content, banners/images and predefined section visibility/order are managed in the dashboard; customers can submit general feedback, separately from moderated product ratings; order creator and lifecycle actions (status changes, follow-up, closure) must be attributable to a user/time; closures record success/failure and a reason; refused/non-received orders and defect exchanges must be distinguishable. Proposed `homepage_sections`, `banners`, `customer_feedback` and append-only `order_activity_events` support these requirements without deciding the remaining display-name and guest-submission policies.

Approved staff permission matrix (2026-10-29): `super_admin` retains all current permissions, including staff/role management, settings and activity logs. `manager` receives customer, catalog, price, supplier, homepage/FAQ, order/revision/payment/shipment/return/refund/expense, review/feedback moderation, contact/ticket and reporting permissions. `operator` receives order/payment/shipment/return and contact/ticket permissions; customers and review moderation are denied by default under least privilege. Staff roles are assigned from this fixed matrix; the dashboard does not provide arbitrary permission grants.

Customer management implementation: customers and saved addresses are soft-archived, and historical orders remain linked and visible. Archiving a customer does not deactivate a linked `users` login account; customer checkout must require an active, non-archived customer record. Decide before customer checkout whether archiving should also block sign-in or only order placement. Proposed default: preserve sign-in and block customer-owned actions while the linked customer record is archived.

Newly confirmed: historical orders and reports preserve all numeric/product details as recorded; dashboard needs weekly/monthly/yearly sales, return, damage and cost/profit views; product ratings remain manageable; customers need Contact Us and support tickets. Product markup percentage is calculated against per-unit factory, inbound-delivery, packaging and advertising costs; the administrator still sets the regular price manually, with customer shipping separate. Reporting timezone is `Africa/Cairo`, month starts on day 1, and week starts Saturday (Saturday-Friday). Ticket notifications are stored for in-dashboard viewing without realtime push.

Latest storefront clarification: an authorized administrator controls regular/base price and optional sale-price edits; prices and discounts are entered separately for each sellable variant/size. When discounted, both prices are shown on the storefront, and both plus actual charged price/discount are preserved in price history and order snapshots. Staff can manage About Us FAQ questions and answers from the dashboard. Guests can browse, add to cart and view checkout; require registration/login only before order placement, preserving cart and checkout choices and restoring checkout afterwards. Product comparison, newsletter signup and blog are explicitly excluded from the current MVP. Simple Favorites are approved: one heart toggle saving a product per customer, without named lists, variants, quantities or sharing. Full Wishlist behavior is out of scope. Sale scheduling remains unresolved below.

## Newly identified unresolved decisions from the schema review

| Decision | Why unresolved | Proposed default for review |
|---|---|---|
| How long may a proposed order revision remain pending, and what happens on timeout, rejection, or a newer revision? | Approval/expiry state affects fulfillment and customer contract. | Set expiry duration only after approval; on reject/timeout, pause and require staff resolution or cancel only under an approved customer policy. No default cancellation is assumed. |
| Which approval channels and evidence are acceptable, including consent evidence retention? | Legal enforceability and privacy obligations are unknown. | Record exact revision, channel, time and minimal evidence; approve channels and retention with legal/business owner. |
| What tax snapshot fields/calculation rules apply, if any? | Tax jurisdiction, VAT inclusion, invoice format and rounding have not been decided. | Store nullable tax snapshot/details and totals; do not calculate, collect or claim tax compliance until approved. |
| How is carrier cost evidenced, who bears COD/collection fees, and what makes a variance explained/approved? | Shipping charge, cost, fees, collection and remittance differ. | Capture shipment estimates and statement actuals separately; require reason and actor for every variance; approve thresholds/escalation. |
| Can carrier remittances be split or netted across periods, and may partial remittance exceed statement net due? | Settlement lifecycle and cash reconciliation depend on carrier practice. | Permit multiple partial remittances; block over-remittance unless explicitly recorded as advance/adjustment. Confirm carrier contracts. |
| How should payment gateway settlements and fees enter cash/accounting records? | Current payment transaction tracking does not include gateway payout reconciliation. | Add a gateway settlement module only once providers and accounting expectations are selected. |
| What exact reasons and transitions are allowed when closing an order, and what qualifies as successful closure? | The system must record successful/failed closure and a reason, but examples do not define the full reason list or whether success means delivery, customer acceptance, or another business milestone. | Configure reason codes (including customer refusal/non-receipt) and require a reason plus actor/time; approve success criteria and reopening policy before implementation.
| How should a product-defect exchange be fulfilled and financially reconciled? | An exchange after delivery differs from a failed delivery, but replacement-order linkage, price difference, shipping responsibility, refund eligibility and any off-system supplier claim are undecided. | Create a linked exchange case and preserve the original order; approve replacement, refund/adjustment and shipping rules separately.
| Can guests submit general customer feedback, and what customer name (if any) should appear publicly? | Dashboard approval and visibility controls are confirmed, but guest eligibility and public display-name policy are not specified. | Allow dashboard moderation and visibility toggles; do not expose customer identity until its display policy is approved. |
| Can a customer change the email used to sign in, and what verification is required? | Email is an authentication identifier; accepting an unverified replacement can lock out or redirect account ownership. | Keep email read-only in the customer profile until an email verification flow and behavior for duplicate/unverified addresses are approved. |

**S1-09 implementation gate (2026-10-30):** dashboard moderation, rejection, visibility and activity history can be used with already-created records. Do not add public submission endpoints until the owner approves the review scale and purchase-eligibility rule, and separately decides guest feedback eligibility and public display-name behavior. The current schema stores a numeric `rating` and moderation fields but does not itself define those policies.
| Do homepage design slots require distinct desktop/mobile images, and what are the crop/dimensions/CTA rules? | The user confirmed dashboard-managed homepage images, but detailed responsive asset requirements depend on the selected design files. | Start with manually ordered images per placement and optional schedule; add separate responsive asset fields only if the chosen layout requires them.
| Which event date determines when a sale, return or refund is counted (order placement, delivery, COD collection, return request, refund paid)? | The calendar and timezone are confirmed, but accounting/reporting date basis can shift values between periods. | Make each measure's date basis explicit and show sale and refund/return dates separately until the owner/accountant selects the authoritative basis. |
| How are sales, return, damage and profit percentages defined and what denominators should they use? | “نسبة المبيعات” and return/damage percentages can mean share of orders, units, or value; denominator affects decisions. | Display raw counts and values alongside any percentage; agree on numerator/denominator labels before presenting a KPI as authoritative.
| How should the shared advertising budget be converted into a per-unit price input? | Staff enters an advertising amount per unit in each variant's price record, but there is no approved attribution method for distributing a campaign budget across products. | Treat the entered per-unit value as a manual estimate; keep shared campaign expense allocation separate until approved. |

**Resolved 2026-10-29:** the displayed/saved percentage is markup on total per-unit cost: (regular price − total cost) / total cost × 100, rounded half-up to four decimal places. The regular price remains manually entered; the percentage does not generate or change it. Zero total cost is invalid for this calculation. The initial price version does not require a reason; later price-version changes require one for audit context. Shared advertising attribution remains open as a separate reporting question.
| What are the ticket response-time commitments, retention period and exact staff permissions? | In-dashboard non-realtime notifications are confirmed, but service-level, privacy and retention policies are not. | Notify assigned/authorized staff in the dashboard and keep internal notes private; approve access matrix and retention before implementation. |
| Should product sale prices have scheduled start/end times or be changed manually by staff? | The owner confirmed base and discounted prices but did not specify timed campaigns or auto-expiry. | Store an optional sale price per variant and manually set/clear it; show both prices while the sale price is present. Add start/end scheduling only if explicitly requested. |

## Confirmed MVP scope decisions

- Selected static storefront starting points: `emarket/index.html` (home), `emarket/category.html` (category listing), `emarket/product-v2.html` (product detail), and `emarket/about-us-4.html` (About). These are design/template selections, not yet Blade implementations.
- Supplier procurement, inventory, supplier accounting reconciliation and automated shipping-provider integrations are outside the current scope. Keep manual shipment status/cost, returns and order financial history.

Additional unresolved design questions:

| Decision | Why unresolved | Proposed default for review |
|---|---|---|
| Which supplier cost should drive the official customer price when a product has several supplier options, and should a supplier change trigger recalculation? | Supplier-specific costs vary while the public product price is maintained separately; supplier selection is per order line. | Keep the published product price fixed unless staff creates and obtains approval for a customer-facing price revision; report the selected supplier cost as the actual cost for that line. |

Approve state names/transitions before wiring dashboard actions. |
| Who bears return-to-sender and failed-delivery carrier charges, and can any amount be collected from the customer? | The carrier charge must be recorded as an actual cost; customer liability/chargeback is a separate customer-policy decision. | Record the carrier expense against the shipment leg and do not add it to a customer balance or refund deduction without an approved policy. |

**Resolved by owner 2026-11-03:** website customer delivery charges vary by governorate and are configured by the owner in protected shipping settings. Actual rates are operational data and are not seeded as assumptions; checkout blocks any governorate with no configured rate. The initial order status is `new` (جديد); later order status transitions remain unresolved for S2-04.

## Decisions to resolve before dependent workflows

- MySQL configuration and MySQL 8.0.30 server version were verified before the MVP migrations; this is no longer a schema blocker. Recheck the deployment environment separately before release.
- **Resolved 2026-10-29:** `products.sku` is the aggregate/product-family code; `product_variants.sku` uniquely identifies each sellable size/color variant. Both remain required and unique, with no schema change.
- **Resolved 2026-10-29:** profit rate basis is markup on cost, rounded half-up to four decimal percentage places; it is calculated from the manually entered regular price and cost inputs. No automatic selling-price calculation is enabled. The per-unit advertising input is entered manually; shared campaign-budget allocation remains undecided.
- Before related behavior is implemented, resolve order status transitions, approval expiry/evidence, return/refund and failed-delivery charge treatment, and authoritative reporting dates/KPI denominators. Supplier procurement, supplier settlements and inventory are outside the current scope and would need a separate approval to add.
