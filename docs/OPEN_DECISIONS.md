# Open business and data decisions

## Status key

- **Approved requirements** are only the business requirements explicitly stated in the user brief and repeated in [database/README.md](database/README.md). They are not accounting/legal policy.
- **Proposed defaults** below are design recommendations for review. None has been accepted unless the user explicitly says so.
- **Unresolved decisions** are questions without a selected policy; schema fields preserve facts without deciding their legal/accounting interpretation.

## Approved requirements (not open)

MySQL; Laravel/Blade modular monolith; Arabic and English; EGP default; customer account required before placing a website order; guests may browse, add to cart and view checkout; Laravel session admin authentication; simple and variable products; product-family SKU generated from the English product name on creation and stable afterward, plus unique sellable variant SKU; products may have multiple factory/showroom supplier options, with a supplier selected per order line; product-level manually set available/unavailable status; no procurement or inventory modules; manual shipping initially with future carrier integrations; DECIMAL money; historical snapshots; explicit approval when final order price/products/quantities change; independent order/payment/shipment statuses; preserved financial and operational audit trail; transaction/row locking for concurrency; no hard deletion of historical financial/operational records.

Latest clarified supplier scope: each product may link to multiple factory/showroom suppliers and staff selects the suitable supplier for each order line. The application does not manage purchase orders, receipts, payables or inventory. Product-level storefront availability is manually set to available/unavailable; website payment is COD only; InstaPay is a staff-entered option only for Facebook/Instagram orders and includes a proof image plus payment verification status; product rating/comment is moderated in the dashboard before publication; track per-order product cost and shipping, packaging and advertising operating costs.

These do not approve a tax rate/treatment, legal retention period, accounting method, refund eligibility, approval expiry/channel, or any other policy listed below.

Additional confirmed operational requirements: homepage content, banners/images and predefined section visibility/order are managed in the dashboard; customers can submit general feedback, separately from moderated product ratings; order creator and lifecycle actions (status changes, follow-up, closure) must be attributable to a user/time; closures record success/failure and a reason; refused/non-received orders and defect exchanges must be distinguishable. Proposed `homepage_sections`, `banners`, `customer_feedback` and append-only `order_activity_events` support these requirements without deciding the remaining display-name and guest-submission policies.

**Resolved by owner 2026-11-04 — order lifecycle:** order states progress only `new` → `confirmed` → `shipped` → `received`. `received` is the successful terminal status and requires explicit staff confirmation that the customer received the order. Failure closure is a separate closure projection, not an additional order status: it may be recorded from any non-received, not-already-closed operational status and requires one of `customer_cancelled`, `customer_refused`, `customer_unreachable`, `incorrect_address`, `product_unavailable`, `duplicate_order`, or `other` (with explanatory text for `other`). A failed closure leaves the operational status unchanged; reopening clears only the current failure projection while preserving both closure and reopening events. Payment and shipment states remain independent. Defect exchanges after delivery remain separate return/exchange cases.

**Resolved by owner — current return, shipping and exchange policy:** accept a return only when the product is damaged or has a clear manufacturing defect, and the customer returns it unused. The owner bears shipping cost for a return, a shipment the customer does not accept, and an exchange. After successful delivery, a qualifying damaged-return request reopens the same order under the `damaged_return` after-sales workflow: arrange carrier pickup from the customer, receive/inspect the item, exchange it, and follow delivery of the replacement. Do not create a new order or new sale, and preserve the original delivered status/event and order snapshot in history. Add exchange shipping cost to the original order's expenses.

Approved staff permission matrix (2026-10-29): `super_admin` retains all current permissions, including staff/role management, settings and activity logs. `manager` receives customer, catalog, price, supplier, homepage/FAQ, order/revision/payment/shipment/return/refund/expense, review/feedback moderation, contact/ticket and reporting permissions. `operator` receives order/payment/shipment/return and contact/ticket permissions; customers and review moderation are denied by default under least privilege. Staff roles are assigned from this fixed matrix; the dashboard does not provide arbitrary permission grants.

Customer management implementation: customers and saved addresses are soft-archived, and historical orders remain linked and visible. Archiving a customer does not deactivate a linked `users` login account; customer checkout must require an active, non-archived customer record. Decide before customer checkout whether archiving should also block sign-in or only order placement. Proposed default: preserve sign-in and block customer-owned actions while the linked customer record is archived.

Newly confirmed: historical orders and reports preserve all numeric/product details as recorded; dashboard needs weekly/monthly/yearly sales, return, damage and cost/profit views; product ratings remain manageable; customers need Contact Us and support tickets. Product markup percentage is calculated against per-unit factory, inbound-delivery, packaging and advertising costs; the administrator still sets the regular price manually, with customer shipping separate. Reporting timezone is `Africa/Cairo`, month starts on day 1, and week starts Saturday (Saturday-Friday). Ticket notifications are stored for in-dashboard viewing without realtime push.

Latest storefront clarification: an authorized administrator controls regular/base price and optional sale-price edits; prices and discounts are entered separately for each sellable variant/size. When discounted, both prices are shown on the storefront, and both plus actual charged price/discount are preserved in price history and order snapshots. Staff can manage About Us FAQ questions and answers from the dashboard. Guests can browse, add to cart and view checkout; require registration/login only before order placement, preserving cart and checkout choices and restoring checkout afterwards. Product comparison, newsletter signup and blog are explicitly excluded from the current MVP. Simple Favorites are approved: one heart toggle saving a product per customer, without named lists, variants, quantities or sharing. Full Wishlist behavior is out of scope. Sale scheduling remains unresolved below.

## Confirmed MVP scope decisions

- **Superseded design choice:** the initial eMarket page mapping was replaced by the owner's later Rapidshop direction. Active storefront views use the HS Shop Rapidshop Blade layout for home, category, product details, About, cart, checkout and account pages. Keep `emarket/` as a static reference only; do not route customers to its old demo product page. The legacy `/product-v2.html` route and Blade copy were removed in S2-11.
- Supplier procurement, inventory, supplier accounting reconciliation and automated shipping-provider integrations are outside the current scope. Keep manual shipment status/cost, returns and order financial history.

**S2-06 implementation default (not a separate business-policy approval):** a rejected proof remains in history and may be followed by a new proof; only one proof may await review per payment. Acceptance means the reviewer confirms the transfer for the full order amount. The partial/mismatched amount policy remains unresolved in the decision table above.

**S2-07 implementation defaults (not accounting or customer-policy approval):** manual outbound attempts can be created only while order status is `shipped` and start at `picked_up` to reflect carrier handoff; staff may assign a carrier and update its shipment only while order status is `shipped`; one active attempt per order; a retry follows `cancelled` or `returned_to_sender`; a delivered order cannot be shipped again. Customer shipping fee on an attempt allocates the order's existing fee and cannot increase it; expected COD is the unpaid order total less COD already recorded, and is zero after accepted InstaPay. The UI records actual collection and shows actual minus expected; these facts do not update order/payment status or create carrier settlement/cash entries.

**S2-09 implementation defaults (not policy approval):** ticket statuses are `open`, `in_progress`, `waiting_customer`, `resolved`, and `closed`; staff may choose a status manually and each change appends an event. The existing `support_tickets.manage` permission gates staff actions, and assignees must be active staff with this permission. Logged-in customers can create and view only their own tickets; they can see public replies and status events only. Internal notes and their attachments are staff-only. Contact Us remains available to guests and its message can be converted once to a ticket, but no email/SMS reply is sent and the guest has no portal access to the converted ticket until a verified account-linking policy is approved. In-dashboard notifications use the existing database notification table without realtime delivery. Attachment defaults are at most three JPEG/PNG/WebP/PDF files per message, 5 MB each, stored privately and streamed only after authorization.

**S2-10 reporting defaults (provisional implementation choices, not owner/accounting approval):** dashboard “today” uses `Africa/Cairo`; report weeks run Saturday through Friday, months from day 1, and years follow the calendar year. The orders cohort is selected by `orders.placed_at` and values come from the currently applied immutable `order_revisions` snapshot. Received/failure event counts use `order_activity_events.occurred_at`; returns and damaged returns use `returns.requested_at`; paid refunds use `refunds.refunded_at`; recorded COD collection uses outbound `shipments.delivered_at`; general overhead uses posted, unlinked `expenses.expense_date`. Direct costs are attached to the order-placement cohort and actual/estimate totals are shown separately; the provisional selected basis prefers active actual entries for each order/item/shipment/cost-type group and otherwise uses estimates. The displayed “estimated contribution” is successful order value before tax less selected direct costs and completed refunds for that order cohort; it excludes general overhead and is not accounting profit or taxable income. No percentages are shown because their denominators are unresolved. These date bases and formula are implementation defaults only.

## S2-08 implementation addendum (2026-11-04)

The following implementation defaults supersede earlier proposed descriptions where they conflict; recommendations remain distinct from owner-approved policy:

- **Owner-approved:** return eligibility is product damage or a clear manufacturing defect, returned unused; the owner bears shipping for returns, failed delivery/non-receipt and exchanges. A delivered order is reopened as the same order in a damaged-return after-sales cycle; original sale snapshot and successful-delivery event remain.
- **Owner-approved:** staff enters the reviewed refund amount manually. The system records a pending refund and only marks it completed after a separate authorized confirmation. It does not calculate or automatically send refunds. Tender, timing, payout evidence and the approval roles are still open.
- **Technical safeguard:** cumulative non-void refund amounts cannot exceed the original order total. Refund tender, payout timing, inspection evidence, and who may approve inspection/refund remain unresolved.
- **Proposed safe default implemented:** exchange sends the exact original product variant and returned quantity. A different variant/product/quantity or any price difference is not enabled because the existing customer-approval rule applies; approve the customer-approval evidence and workflow before enabling substitutions.
- **Technical schema addition approved in this conversation:** nullable shipments.return_id FK and return_items snapshots for replacement variant FK, SKU, product name, variant JSON, and quantity. This is an additive migration; it has not been run against any database.
- **Implemented cost ledger convention:** product cost is entered as a total for an order line and remains separate from operating expense. Actual shipping/packaging/advertising/other direct costs are mirrored into expenses; estimates stay in order_cost_entries. Corrections are negative linked reversals. Reports must not sum both the ledger and its linked expense mirrors.
- **Still unresolved:** structured supplier identity for per-order product cost, whether and how customers authorize any substitute, refund tender/timing/evidence and approver, inspection evidence/authority, tax/accounting recognition, retention, and report date/percentage definitions. Do not convert these defaults into business requirements.
